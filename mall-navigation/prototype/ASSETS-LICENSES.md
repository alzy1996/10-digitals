# Third-party assets

The v0.4 "real mall" look uses these CC0 1.0 (public domain) assets from Poly Haven. CC0 needs no attribution; they
are credited here anyway. They are converted once by `tools/build_real_assets.py` (WebP textures, a 512 × 256 RGBE
HDRI) and served from our own origin (`public/assets/real/`), never from a CDN.

| File | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `marble.webp`, `marble-n.webp`, `marble-r.webp` | [Marble Tiles](https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_tiles) (diffuse, normal GL, roughness, 1k) | CC0 1.0 |
| `plaster.webp`, `plaster-n.webp` | [Painted Plaster Wall](https://polyhaven.com/a/painted_plaster_wall) (diffuse, normal GL, 1k) | CC0 1.0 |
| `wood.webp` | [Brown Planks 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_planks_03) (diffuse, 1k) | CC0 1.0 |
| `arcade.hdr` | [Leadenhall Market](https://polyhaven.com/a/leadenhall_market) (HDRI, 1k, downsampled to 512 × 256) | CC0 1.0 |
