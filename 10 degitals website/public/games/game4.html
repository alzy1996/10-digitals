OMAN FLOUR MILLS — FLEET COORDINATOR TRAINING

You are the Fleet Coordinator on the 06:00–14:00 shift. Four departments post demand cards: PRODUCTION needs grain at the silo intake, PROCUREMENT / STORES needs supply containers docked, and DELIVERY needs the right trucks at the right bays — plus weight-out jobs for trucks already loaded on site.

Dispatch trucks from OUTSIDE STAGING. Every truck must pass the GATE and the single WEIGHBRIDGE (twice: tare and gross). One scale, one truck — your dispatch order is the queue. Roads are single-lane: no overtaking, and a stopped truck blocks everyone behind it.

Match the truck to the cargo and the size to the order — a tipper cannot carry bags, and a 7 MT truck cannot carry a 45 MT SO. Outbound trucks need a Delivery Note before the gate: no document, no movement.

Late demand = penalty + an angry department. 5 missed deadlines ends the shift. Standing trucks burn demurrage OMR. Read the PROCESS BOOK (top right) any time.
