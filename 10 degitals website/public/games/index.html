OMAN FLOUR MILLS — FEED MILLS DEPT

OFM LOGISTICS ARCADE

Four 3D games built from our real daily work — loading lines, the weighbridge, the warehouse, and fleet management. 5 truck types (grain tipper, supplies container, bulk outbound, reefer, flat bed) in 7 / 15 / 45 / 60 MT classes, real single-file traffic (no overtaking), FIFO stock discipline, the HSE layer and the full SO → DN → gate pass document chain. Runs fully offline, on PC or phone — learn the operation while you climb the high score.

GAME 1 — DISPATCHING

Loading Line Rush

Trucks are queuing at the gate with their SO numbers. You are the dispatcher: send each truck to a free loading line before the driver loses patience. Bran, barley feed, flour — keep the lines moving, keep the queue short, earn your OMR.

GAME 2 — RECEIVING

Weighbridge Master

Barley, wheat and corn trucks are rolling in. Weigh them, check the quality report (moisture and impurity — accept or reject!), then send each load to the correct silo. One weighbridge, many trucks — do not let the yard jam.

GAME 3 — WAREHOUSE — NEW

Forklift Warehouse 3D

Drive the forklift on the dispatch shift. Every pallet carries a product code and a batch age, and the rule is oldest batch first — the newest stock is always the closest, which is exactly why it is the wrong one. Break FIFO and the old batch ages out and gets written off. Forks down to travel, horn at the walkway, DN then gate pass before the truck moves.

GAME 4 — FLEET MANAGEMENT — NEW

Fleet Management

You are the fleet coordinator. Production demands raw material trucks, Stores expects supply containers, Delivery posts SOs and weight-out jobs — and there is only ONE weighbridge. Sequence every truck, avoid road jams, keep the documents flowing. This one teaches the real operation.
