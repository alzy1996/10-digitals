OFM FORKLIFT WAREHOUSE

V2 — FIFO · SAFETY · DOCUMENTS

You are the forklift operator on the Feed Mills dispatch shift. A Sales Order names a product code and a quantity. Pull those pallets out of the racking and load the truck on the DISPATCH DOCK.

  • FIFO — oldest batch first. Every pallet carries a batch age. The newest stock sits nearest the aisle mouth; the oldest is deeper in. Taking the easy one costs you.
  • Right code only. 716 is not 715. A wrong pallet on the truck is a rejected load.
  • Stock ages. A batch past its shelf life is written off — straight out of the ledger.
  • Forks down to travel. Driving with a raised load is an unsafe act.
  • Pedestrians have right of way. Sound the HORN at the walkway. Striking someone ends the shift immediately — no partial credit, ever.
  • Documents. The truck leaves on DN then GATE PASS, in that order. Until it does, demurrage runs.

W A S D or ←↑↓→ drive · SPACE pick / drop · QE forks · H horn · R reset if stuck. On a phone: left stick to drive, right button to pick, fork buttons on the right.
