OMAN FLOUR MILLS — FEED MILL DISPATCH

HOW TO PLAY
1. Trucks arrive and queue at the mill gate, each with an SO number and a product: BRAN (brown), BARLEY FEED (yellow) or FLOUR (white).
2. Tap a waiting truck (in the yard or its chip below), then tap a free loading line (LINE 1–3) to dispatch it.
3. The loader fills the truck — fast dispatches earn bonus points and OMR, and build your combo.
4. Every truck has patience. If it waits too long it leaves angry: you lose your combo and get a strike.
5. 5 strikes = GAME OVER. Each level, trucks arrive faster!
